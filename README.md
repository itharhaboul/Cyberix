# CyberiX 👾

> **Status: Work in Progress** 🛠️
>
> CyberiX is a 2D adventure game currently under development. It combines investigation, action, and mystery in a dark science-fiction environment. The current version is an early prototype, with new gameplay mechanics and story elements planned for future updates.

## The Idea 🕯️

Something went terribly wrong inside an advanced research center, leaving it abandoned and filled with dangerous robotic systems.

You play as a detective sent to investigate the mysterious incident. As you explore the facility, you will encounter hostile robots, uncover hidden clues, and gradually discover the truth behind what happened.

But be careful: not everything inside the research center is what it seems..🫥!

**Your mission is to investigate the facility, collect evidence, survive its dangers, and uncover the mystery.**

## Core Gameplay 🎮

CyberiX combines three main gameplay elements:

- **Investigation:** Explore the research center and collect clues to uncover the truth.
- **Action:** Face different types of hostile robots, each with its own behavior.
- **Mystery:** Discover the secrets behind the abandoned facility and the system controlling it.

## Current Features 🎯

- Player movement using the arrow keys.
- Shooting mechanics using the Space key.
- Multiple enemy types with different behaviors.
- Enemy health and collision detection.
- Player and enemy bullet systems.
- Score system.
- Basic enemy respawning.
- Separate game assets and MVC architecture.

## What's Next? 🔍

The development roadmap includes:

- Collectible clues and an investigation system.
- A laboratory environment with interactive elements.
- A Control Room that becomes accessible after collecting the required clues.
- Multiple rooms and environments.
- New enemy behaviors and challenges.
- Improved graphics and visual effects.
- Sound effects and background music.
- A deeper storyline involving the mysterious AI controlling the facility.

**The investigation has only just begun...** 🕯️.
