#  CyberiX
>  **Status: Work in Progress** 👾
> 
> CyberiX is still under development. The current version represents the beginning of the project, and more features, improvements, and gameplay elements will be added in future updates.

## The Idea🕯️

The story takes place in an abandoned research center where something went wrong.
You play as a detective who enters the facility to investigate the mystery behind it. However, the place is not empty. Dangerous robots and monsters are waiting inside.
Your mission is to survive, face the enemies, and uncover what happened in the research center.

## Current Features 🎯

- Player movement using the arrow keys.
- Shooting bullets using the Space key.
- Enemy health and collision detection.
- Score system.
- Basic enemy respawning.

##  What's Next 🫥?

The project is still evolving. Planned improvements include:
> New enemy types and behaviors.
> Multiple levels with increasing difficulty.
> Clues and rewards to make the story more interactive.
>  Improved graphics and game environment.
> Sound effects and background music.
